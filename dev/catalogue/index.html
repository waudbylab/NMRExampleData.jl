titration2d_fir_nbox

FIR-Nbox 2D 1H,15N SOFAST-HMQC titration (600 MHz, 315 K)

Titration of FIR RRM1-RRM2 with Nbox peptide

spec = exampledata("titration2d_fir_nbox")

Loads as a vector of 11 spectra, in the order given in dataset.toml. Includes the raw data: load it with exampledata("titration2d_fir_nbox"; fid=true). Matched to 11 sample records. No pulse programme annotations.

Credit: Chris Waudby, Andres Ramos, Cyprian Cukier

Cite: Cukier, C., Hollingworth, D., Martin, S. et al. Molecular basis of FIR-mediated c-myc transcriptional control. Nat Struct Mol Biol 17, 1058–1064 (2010). https://doi.org/10.1038/nsmb.1883
